# FoodToru Website

This directory contains the landing page, privacy policy, and terms and conditions for the FoodToru app.

## Files

- **index.html** - Main landing page with app features, how it works, and pricing
- **privacy.html** - Privacy policy explaining data collection, storage, and user rights
- **terms.html** - Terms and conditions for using the app

## Features

### Landing Page (index.html)
- Modern, responsive design
- Hero section with call-to-action
- Features showcase
- How it works section
- Pricing information
- Navigation to privacy and terms pages

### Privacy Policy (privacy.html)
- Comprehensive privacy policy
- Data collection and usage information
- Security measures
- Third-party services (Claude AI, Apple CloudKit)
- User rights (GDPR, CCPA compliance)
- Contact information

### Terms & Conditions (terms.html)
- Complete terms of service
- Usage guidelines
- Medical disclaimer
- Liability limitations
- User responsibilities
- Contact information

## Customization

Before deploying, update the following:

1. **Contact Information:**
   - Replace `support@foodtoru.com` with your actual support email
   - Replace `privacy@foodtoru.com` with your actual privacy email
   - Replace `legal@foodtoru.com` with your actual legal email

2. **Jurisdiction:**
   - In `terms.html`, replace `[Your Jurisdiction]` with your actual jurisdiction

3. **App Store Link:**
   - In `index.html`, update the App Store download link when available

4. **Company Information:**
   - Update copyright year if needed
   - Add company name if different from FoodToru
